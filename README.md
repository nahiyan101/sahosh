# সাহস · Sahosh

A private, Bangla-first web tool that helps young people recognise, document
and respond to technology-facilitated gender-based violence.

**[▶ Open the live prototype](https://nahiyan101.github.io/sahosh/)**

Built by **Team Voices Beyond the Frame** for the **AI & TGBV Hackathon 2026**,
organised by Share-Net Bangladesh.

---

## The problem

In Bangladesh, self-styled "mobile journalists" film young women in public
without consent and publish the footage on Facebook pages with captions
inviting ridicule. The posts go viral and the women are shamed by thousands
of strangers in the comments.

A 2024 BBS–UNFPA survey found nearly 89% of women social media users in
Bangladesh have faced online violence, with women aged 18–30 most affected.
Around 75% never report it. Free support services exist, but a national
survey found 88% of survivors did not know helpline 109 existed.

The gap is the first hour. When she finds the post, she often does not know
that publishing her image to shame her is a violation, what evidence to save,
or who to contact — while the post keeps spreading.

## What Sahosh does

1. **Recognise** — names the type of abuse, cites the law, and makes clear it is not her fault
2. **Act** — an ordered checklist to preserve evidence and lock down her accounts
3. **Document** — sorts the abusive comments by type and generates a one-page evidence report
4. **Respond** — drafts a formal Bangla complaint for PCSW or a General Diary
5. **Support** — connects her to free existing services: 109, 999, Kaan Pete Roi
6. **Bystander mode** — guides a friend who found the post on what actually helps

## Privacy by design

No account, no login, nothing stored. Her photo is never uploaded or included
in the report. A quick-exit button (or the Esc key) clears the page instantly.
Sahosh never reports, replies or files anything on her behalf — she decides
everything.

## Prototype notice

Every scenario, post, comment and person in this demonstration is invented.
No real survivor's images, posts or messages were used at any stage.
Classification is simulated locally in the browser. This is not legal advice
and not a substitute for the police, a lawyer or a counsellor.

## Built with

A single self-contained HTML file. No build step, no dependencies, no tracking.
